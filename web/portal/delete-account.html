Delete your Burjex Prime account

How to request deletion, and what happens to your data

Request deletion from the app

  1. Open the Burjex Prime app and sign in.
  2. Open the menu, then Account › Delete Account.
  3. Choose a reason, confirm your password, and submit.

You can also do this in a browser — no app install needed.

Open the client portal

If you can no longer sign in

Email support@burjexprime.com from the address registered on your account and ask us to delete it. We may ask you to verify your identity before we act — this protects you from someone else closing your account.

What happens next

Your request is held for 30 days, during which you can cancel it from the app. After that your personal data is erased. This cannot be undone.

If you hold a balance, an open trade or a withdrawal in progress, our team will contact you to settle it before your account closes. You do not need to settle anything before submitting the request.
